Shipping & Logistics Policy.

Logistics dispatch framework, site delivery coordination, and freight handling for heavy capital machinery across industrial corridors.

Operational Delivery Notice: Standard freight parameters for M/s. Pukhraj Hastimal & Co. Specific transit terms (FOB, Ex-Works, or CIF) and transit insurance are negotiated individually for each commercial purchase order.

1. Primary Delivery Hub & Destination

Our verified logistics network coordinates direct dispatches to client industrial corridors. An example of our primary consignee delivery hub is documented in Purchase Order Ref: PH/001/26-27:

Consignee Delivery Hub:
Indian Emulsifiers Limited
E 10 Lote Parshuram Industrial Zone, MIDC Tal – Khed, Ratnagiri – 415722, Maharashtra

2. Heavy Freight & Specialized Transport

Capital equipment—such as 850 KG/HR steam boilers, transformer units, large-diameter cooling towers, and multi-tube heat exchangers—is dispatched via dedicated heavy-axle hydraulic trailers, flatbed carriers, or specialized logistics skids.

3. Unloading & Site Erection

Unless expressly contracted as a turnkey erection package (such as Line Item 08 of PO Ref: PH/001/26-27 for plant electrification and cable trays), site unloading, crane hire, civil foundations, and terminal connections remain the responsibility of the purchaser.

4. Returns & Acceptance Inspection

Due to the bespoke engineering nature of heavy capital equipment, returns are governed strictly by factory pre-dispatch inspection protocols (FAT) and formal delivery acceptance reports agreed upon in the purchase contract.

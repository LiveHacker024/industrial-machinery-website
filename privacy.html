Privacy Policy.

Information disclosure explaining how corporate and technical data submitted through our quotation forms is processed and protected.

Legal Template Notice: This document represents a standard B2B industrial privacy disclosure for M/s. Pukhraj Hastimal & Co. Specific legal formulations require final review by legal counsel before statutory certification.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a Request for Quotation (RFQ) or contact form through our website, we collect only the business parameters necessary to process your equipment enquiry:

  • Representative name and designation
  • Company / Purchasing enterprise name
  • Corporate email address and direct telephone / mobile number
  • Machinery of interest, required capacity, and site delivery parameters

2. Purpose of Information Processing

All submitted details are utilized strictly for industrial equipment evaluation, quotation generation, engineering feasibility reviews, and direct commercial correspondence. We do not sell, rent, or trade your corporate information to third-party marketing services.

3. Client-Side Data Dispatch

Our RFQ dispatcher operates transparently by formatting inquiries directly into your default email application addressed to our procurement desk (pukhrajhastimal@gmail.com). No intermediate public cloud databases store your proprietary technical data without explicit mutual agreement.

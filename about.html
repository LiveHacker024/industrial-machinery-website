COMPANY BACKGROUND & PROFILE

M/s. PUKHRAJ HASTIMAL & CO.

M/s. Pukhraj Hastimal & Co. is an established industrial equipment supplier, thermal engineering provider, and electrical distribution contractor operating from Mumbai, Maharashtra. We coordinate the supply, technical verification, and on-site delivery of heavy industrial assets for manufacturing and chemical facilities across India.

Our business specializes in multi-discipline industrial scopes: steam generation boilers, thermal insulation with aluminium weather cladding, high-tension (HT) vacuum circuit breaker substations, step-down transformers, automatic power factor correction (APFC) panels, process chillers, cooling towers, and rotary compressed air systems.
